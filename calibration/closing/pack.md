# Closing-Turn Calibration Pack

*Calibrating the closing-turn quality axis of an AI facilitator against expert judgement.*

## What we are asking you to do

Harmonica is an AI facilitator that runs 1:1 conversations and ends each one with a *closing turn*. We score every closing turn automatically (an LLM-as-judge against a rubric) to catch quality regressions when we change the prompt. The problem: that judge encodes our *guess* at a good close, not an expert facilitator's. Your labels recalibrate it.

For each close below: read the participant's contributions, then the AI's closing turn, and mark it **Strong** or **Weak** with **one line of why**. Ten to fifteen minutes total. One honest line of *why* beats a paragraph.

## Our working definition of a strong close

Tell us if you would draw the line differently — that feedback is as valuable as the ratings.

> A **strong close** does two things: (1) it reflects the participant's **own specific points** back to them, the actual things they said, not a generic thank-you; and (2) it offers a quick **checkpoint** to confirm or correct ("did I capture this right?", "anything to add before I pass this on?") before treating the conversation as finished.
>
> A **weak close** either (a) summarizes but never checks, or (b) — after the person has clearly signalled they are done — punts back a generic *"would you like to continue, or shall I summarize?"* instead of reflecting-and-checking.
